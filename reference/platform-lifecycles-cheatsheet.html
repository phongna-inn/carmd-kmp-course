Platform Lifecycles

Compressed from Lesson 03 (Android) and Lesson 04 (iOS). Sequences were observed on an Android 16 emulator (targetSdk 36) and an iOS 26.2 simulator.

Rule: never keep long-lived state (connections, scans) in the Activity. It belongs in a ViewModel (Lesson 08).

Rules: app-level work (background monitoring) hooks the scene/app level, never view callbacks. A suspended app gets no callback when it's killed, so save on entering the background.
